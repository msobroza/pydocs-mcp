# ADR 0025 — Scoring the end of the turn budget, and the tiered judge behind the deterministic needle check

**Status:** Proposed — written with the turn-efficiency spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-ask-turn-efficiency-program-design.md`), committed beside it in the documentation PR and reviewed by the owner there before the compare verb's acceptance rule is coded. It records decisions Q7, Q9, Q18, Q19″, Q21, Q22″, Q35, Q38 and Q43 of the owner's decision log, and Q26 and Q48(b) as overridden by the Round 5 amendments, Q51–Q60 and the Round 5d–5g wording; the log is committed as `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-ask-turn-efficiency-decisions.md` in the same documentation PR, beside this ADR and the spec (Q50.1). ·
**Date:** 2026-09-25 · **Phase:** eval (the turn-efficiency ladder, after 0.8.1; Unreleased at HEAD `87ac5d5b`)

- **Decision area:** what the eval records when a question reaches its turn budget; how a Finalized answer and every other unanswered outcome are counted; which check gates correctness; how the Jev judge is pinned, when an escalation judge is consulted, how the alignment labels the judges are fitted to are produced, how reference answers are written, and how every judge is kept out of the loop it grades.
- **Siblings:** ADR 0012 (the rule-based score taxonomy and failure taxonomy the new outcomes extend), ADR 0016 (the baseline campaign's two-stage design with pre-registered analysis, whose paired tests the penalised mean feeds), ADR 0023 (needed and needless calls: the product side of the same program).

Vocabulary is the repo glossary (`CONTEXT.md`): *Needle*, *Turn*, *Turns-to-answer*, *Budget exhaustion*, *Finalized answer*, *Turns after needle*.

## Context

A question can end silently on LangGraph's canned sentinel, and today neither the product nor the eval books that as its own outcome. `turn_budget.py:27-30` maps `max_agent_turns` (`_DEFAULT_MAX_AGENT_TURNS = 12`, `python/pydocs_mcp/retrieval/config/ask_your_docs_models.py:46`) to an even `recursion_limit`, so the prebuilt agent never raises `GraphRecursionError`: it replaces the last tool-calling reply with "Sorry, need more steps to process this request." (langgraph 1.2.8, `chat_agent_executor.py:631,689,716`). The eval binding's translation of `GraphRecursionError` into `TurnBudgetExceededError` (`python/pydocs_mcp/harness/ask_your_docs/binding.py:467-468`) therefore never fires on that path; the sentinel is returned as the answer, stored in chat history, and counted as answered. The eval's timeout wrapper folds a killed run and a capped run into one sentinel trajectory (`benchmarks/src/pydocs_eval/optimize/ask_binding.py:398-405`), and the usage sidecar carries no `finish_reason` (`model_usage.py:133-150`), so a timed-out run, a starved reply and an empty answer are indistinguishable after the fact. The answer text itself is never persisted by the campaign (`before_after_arm.py:312` stores only `answer_chars`). Correctness rests on `gold_substring_all` (`benchmarks/src/pydocs_eval/optimize/rubric/gates.py:145`) and `gold_recall` (`benchmarks/src/pydocs_eval/optimize/rubric/checks.py:378`), optimizer gates that reward any mention and cannot tell a commitment from a hedged list. No reference answer exists for repoqa-qa (its gold is a path, a symbol and the function body, `benchmarks/src/pydocs_eval/datasets/repoqa.py:152-164`) or for the chat slice, so no agreement or completeness question can be asked of them. No compare verb exists under `benchmarks/src/pydocs_eval/campaign/` (`before_after_measure`, `before_after_rows` and `before_after_report` only), so the acceptance rule this ADR fixes has no implementation yet.

## Evidence

- On current code no exhaustion occurred: 0/10 chat questions (maximum 8 Turns) and 0/30 tasks in the 2026-09-15 candidate arm; the one recorded instance is the baseline arm's `pyg-team/pytorch_geometric@af0f5f4` task at 12 Turns and 47 answer characters, booked as answered. The candidate arm's 12-Turn task answered on its last call.
- The mechanism is unmeasured, not absent: the only budget test fakes a graph that raises (`tests/harness/ask_your_docs/test_binding.py:263-300`), and the sentinel carries no `usage_metadata`, so the 12th call's tokens are never metered (`model_usage.py:133-137`). The 2026-09-15 baseline arm holds `server_events.jsonl` for every task but neither `model_turns.json` nor `model_usage.json`, so its Turn-based and cost rows cannot be replayed.
- Needle-reached sits at 29/30 on the repoqa slice, so it cannot see a dropped location; 4/30 repoqa answers were plausible-but-wrong stops with 5–9 Turns unused, and 3/10 multi-site chat answers were incomplete. A deterministic grounding gate proposed for this fired false positives on every case it was designed to catch.
- The Jev judge costs about $0.0001 and 0.1–1 s per answer through OpenRouter; identical calls near 0.5 vary by ±0.05, which is why thresholds are bands, not points.
- The agent answers to align on are not on disk: the 2026-09-15 traces kept only `answer_chars`, so the A/A pair regenerates about 120 answers (2 arms × (10 chat `dev` + 20 chat `test` + 30 repoqa `small_dev`)) with the agent under test, and the forced-cap arms add finalized answers later. A single labeller's blind spots would become the ground truth unchecked; two blind labellers of different families expose them as disagreement, and the direction of their disagreements on reference-based questions measures self-preference.
- The OpenRouter listings of 2026-09-25: `openai/gpt-6-luna` at $0.10/M in, $0.50/M out with `reasoning_effort`, `structured_outputs` and `seed`; `openai/gpt-6-astra:batch`; `anthropic/claude-opus-5.5:batch` at $2/M in, $10/M out. A dual-labelled item is estimated at $0.09–0.20, a Luna pass over the alignment set at $0.2–0.8, the 100 references at $2.50–5.50.

## Options considered

- **Count a Finalized answer as answered** — REJECTED: it hides Budget exhaustion, the symptom the ladder exists to remove, and lets a step "win" by answering later.
- **Exclude starved, timed-out and empty runs from the penalised mean** — REJECTED (Q43): a step could then lower the headline by turning exhaustion into starvation or silence; every unanswered outcome costs the same budget + 1, and the outcome tally keeps them apart.
- **Keep the typed error as the exhaustion channel** — REJECTED: a raised error carries no answer, so a Finalized answer could never be scored; exhaustion travels on the `Trajectory` instead, and the error remains for hand-built graphs only.
- **`jev-latest`, or an alias for any judge** — REJECTED: a silent model change moves every threshold; calibration is per question and per model version, and every client asserts the returned model id.
- **Jev as the primary correctness guard** — REJECTED: Jev reads state literally, is exposed to text injected into the answer, and cannot be the arbiter of a ladder that may later put Jev inside the harness.
- **Average a Score's level distribution** — REJECTED: the TypeSafe guidance is to gate on confidence and cut at level midpoints; an average hides an undecided judgment.
- **One labeller with a random owner spot-check** — REJECTED (Q58): a spot-check of 10 cannot see a systematic blind spot, and the labeller's family would be the only independent signal against the chat model's; two blind labellers plus adjudication of every disagreement and of the escalated agreements replace it.
- **A judge that grades text written by its own family** — REJECTED (Round 5d): the agent under test, the reference writer and the judges are three families; where Opus both writes references and labels, a reference-based label counts only when Astra independently agrees.
- **The alignment labeller as the in-band judge** — REJECTED (Round 5e): Astra produces the alignment set and never judges ladder answers; Luna judges in-band answers and is measured against that set, so the two roles cannot certify each other.
- **Skipping `addresses_grader` on the swe-qa slice** (Q19″'s literal list) — REJECTED: the injection defence is slice-independent, and the second alignment pass plants injections into swe-qa answers that need a flag.
- **One κ scale for the Luna floor and the labellers' ceiling** — NOT DECIDED HERE: comparing Luna on Cohen's κ against a 0.90 floor would put both statistics on one scale, but with the label base rates of a needle judge (needle-reached 29/30) 90% raw agreement is a κ near 0.4–0.6, so a κ floor of 0.90 would read `undecided` on most questions and quietly disable the escalation tier. Q53 and Round 5f state the floor as a 90% agreement rate with the labellers' κ as the ceiling, and this ADR keeps that; a single-scale variant is listed under the spec's open owner words and needs the owner's word before ticket 9d codes the thresholds.
- **Import the product's literals into the eval scorer** — REJECTED: `trajectory/` and the new `judge/` package keep a zero-`pydocs_mcp` floor (`trajectory/token_accounting.py:44-48`); the sentinel and the `Not confirmed:` label are mirrored there with a parity test.

## Decision

1. Exhaustion is a field of the run, not an exception. The product detects the prebuilt sentinel (`is_budget_exhausted_reply`) and returns a `Trajectory` with `budget_exhausted = True`; the eval's timeout wrapper marks a killed run `timed_out = True`; the usage sidecar carries each reply's `finish_reason`. The eval books the mutually exclusive outcomes `TIMEOUT`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, `EXHAUSTED_FINALIZED`, `STARVED_REPLY`, `UNANSWERED_EMPTY`, `ANSWERED` (`UNRECORDED` for rows written before this ADR, with a back-fill for the 47-character sentinel at the cap), and a separate `near_cap` flag. A Finalized answer is its own outcome: unanswered for the answered-within-budget rate, `budget + 1` (13) in the penalised Turns-to-answer mean, its answer text stored and scored.
2. Turns-to-answer is reported as the penalised mean (headline) in which every unanswered outcome — `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, `EXHAUSTED_FINALIZED`, `STARVED_REPLY`, `TIMEOUT`, `UNANSWERED_EMPTY` — counts 13, and the answered-only mean beside it, which excludes them all; the outcome tally shows each one (Q43). Turns after needle is reported in Turns, with call counts as companions.
3. **Needle cited** is the gating correctness guard: code extracts every citation (`benchmarks/src/pydocs_eval/datasets/_citations.py:23-30` plus a widened-extension and dotted-name extractor), normalises module and qualname aliases, and exact-matches the gold; on a multi-site record it is true only when every site is cited, with gold-site coverage as its companion and second guard. `gold_substring_all` and `gold_recall` stay reference columns. The A/A noise band the guard uses is computed from this row by the compare verb, which lands before the first paid comparison.
4. Judging is tiered. Jev is second: `addresses_grader` on every slice; `needle_identified` on repoqa; one Noul per gold site plus `completeness` L0–L3 on the chat slice; one Noul per gold file plus `completeness` on swe-qa; `contradicts_reference` reported as agreement on every slice that carries a reference — one request per answer over named-JSON state, behind an owned `JevJudgeClient` with a `FakeJevJudgeClient` (10 s timeout, 2 retries on timeouts and 5xx, cached by input hash including the model pin). The gold-blind `committed_function` Choice is a separate request run only on alignment items, a calibration audit (Q22″.5), never part of scoring. Scores are gated on confidence and cut at level midpoints, never averaged. Outside its review band Jev's verdict stands. Inside the band the answer goes to the Jev escalation judge, `openai/gpt-6-luna` (`judge.escalation.*`; pinned id, highest accepted reasoning effort, structured labels), never back to Jev. Luna's agreement with the alignment label is measured per question as a percent-agreement rate (Q53, Round 5e/5f): Luna is trusted on a question only when that rate is at least `judge.escalation.agreement_floor` (0.90), or at least the labellers' own per-question Cohen's κ where that κ is below 0.90 (the ceiling, Round 5f); otherwise every in-band answer on that question reads `undecided`. Luna's own κ is reported beside the labellers' κ on every question, and a Luna κ above the labellers' κ is flagged, never rewarded. Escalations run only while ladder arms are scored, are cached so an answer is never labelled twice, and are capped at $2 for the program; beyond the cap, in-band answers read `undecided`.
5. The alignment set — the labels Jev's thresholds and review bands are fitted to and Luna's agreement is measured against — is produced by two independent, blind labellers of different families, `openai/gpt-6-astra:batch` and `anthropic/claude-opus-5.5:batch` (`judge.alignment.*`), on items selected actively from real agent answers (Jev-borderline, code-vs-Jev disagreements, planted injections, a random share; the reserved held-out questions excluded); each labeller receives the ground-truth context and never the other's label or Jev's score, and returns a structured verdict, an evidence quote and a confidence level. Agreement is the label. Disagreement goes to the owner with both rationales; an agreed label also goes to the owner when both contradict the code check, either labeller reports low confidence, a planted injection passed both, or it falls in the random audit of agreed items, whose share grows when more than 5% prove wrong (Q59). Per-question Cohen's κ between the labellers is reported and is the ceiling for every judge's agreement target; a low-κ question is rewritten before any judge is trusted on it. A 10-item dual pilot sizes the passes to the $15 sub-budget. The owner adjudicates through a benchmarks-side annotation page (Q60). Astra never judges a ladder answer; neither labeller ever generates one.
6. Reference answers are written from ground truth only — the gold path and symbol and the function body for repoqa-qa, the code-authored gold sites for the chat slice — by `anthropic/claude-opus-5.5:batch` (fallback `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5:batch` only for a row whose Opus batch job failed or timed out, and the row says so — Q56′), stored in `gold.extra["reference_answer"]` with the model id and prompt hash, frozen and versioned, and code-checked: on repoqa the gold path and symbol present and no other file path; on the chat slice every gold site's path and symbol present and no path outside the gold sites; a failing reference is regenerated, and a row still failing after the retry budget is listed for the owner. The owner spot-checks 5 repoqa references and ratifies the 40 chat references with the chat gold. They never reach the agent and never seed alignment answers. Three families stay apart: Qwen is judged, Anthropic writes references, OpenAI judges; because Opus also labels, a reference-based alignment label counts only when Astra independently agrees, and the direction of their disagreements is reported.
7. Every client pins its model id and asserts the response's model field equals the pin, raising with both values otherwise; `jev-1.13` is pinned under `judge.jev.*`, and the `model` of every role block is empty by default so scoring refuses until the deployment YAML sets it. An upgrade of any pin is a YAML change followed by recalibration on the stored alignment set and an A/A re-score; thresholds live per question and per Jev model version, and a missing block refuses to score — so a dataset whose questions have not had their alignment pass refuses until it lands.
8. No Jev question both steers the harness and grades it. Any in-harness Jev use is opt-in, off by default, never gating, and judged by the deterministic check plus the escalation judge; because Jev is excluded from that verdict there is no in-band trigger, so for that experiment alone Luna runs as a full-coverage judge over the arm's answers, priced on its own ledger line when the experiment is scheduled and outside the $2 in-band cap, still refused on any question below its agreement floor (`min(0.90, κ_labellers)` on the percent-agreement scale); its question texts differ from the eval judge's, and the experiment reports Jev-versus-LLM agreement separately for gated and ungated runs, with Jev scoring those answers for that row only.

## Consequences

- A step that answers only by finalizing, or that turns exhaustion into a starved or empty reply, cannot lower the penalised mean; it must reach the Needle sooner.
- The `Trajectory` gains two defaulted fields (`budget_exhausted`, `timed_out`) and the usage sidecar one (`finish_reason`), so every existing harness and constructor is unchanged; `arm.json` and sidecars written before this ADR still load.
- The sentinel and the `Not confirmed:` label are pinned at three sites — the product constant, the eval mirror, and the parity test between them — never imported across the eval's floor.
- A new dataset refuses to score until its own alignment pass lands; pass 1 covers the chat and repoqa questions, pass 2 on the swe-qa pilot answers covers swe-qa-questions.
- Agreement becomes measurable on every slice and completeness on the chat and swe-qa-questions slices (repoqa stays single-needle), at the price of one paid reference batch per draw and a second freeze (the chat records digest moves once when the references land, still before `system_v3.j2`; the repoqa reference file carries its own digest).
- The owner adjudicates about 30–55 items over the ~160 items of both alignment passes (roughly 15–30 disagreements and 15–25 agreement escalations) through the annotation page; that load, not the model cost, bounds how often criteria can be re-run.
- The optimizer keeps its own rubric judge; Jev, Luna, Astra and Opus judge or label the ladder only.
- Calibration is a recurring cost: any criteria edit or pin change reruns the alignment pass before its verdicts are trusted; the $15 sub-budget and the $2 escalation cap bound it.
